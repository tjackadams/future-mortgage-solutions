Privacy notice

This notice explains how Future Mortgage Solutions Ltd handles personal information provided through this website.

Preview note

This is a private-review draft. Confirm the company registration number, registered office, privacy contact and retention periods from company records before public launch.

Who is responsible for your information?

Future Mortgage Solutions Ltd is the controller of personal information submitted through this website. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact faye@futuremortgagesolutions.co.uk.

What we collect

When you use the enquiry form, we collect your name, email address, optional telephone number and the general message you choose to send. Please do not provide financial account details, identity documents, or mortgage-application information through the form.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to respond to your request and to take steps at your request before entering into a service relationship. We do not use this form to collect marketing consent.

Our service providers

The website is hosted using Cloudflare services. Enquiry messages are delivered to Future Mortgage Solutions through Resend. Cloudflare Turnstile may process limited technical information to protect the form from automated abuse. These providers act under their applicable terms and data-processing arrangements.

Retention and your rights

We retain enquiry information only for the period stated in the approved company retention policy. You may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal information, and may object where applicable. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Analytics and cookies

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate website use. The intended configuration does not use non-essential cookies or identify individual visitors. This notice will be updated if that configuration changes.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our website or information-handling practices change. The current version will always be available at this address.
